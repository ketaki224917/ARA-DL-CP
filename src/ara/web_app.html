Research Studio

Give ARA a research topic and, optionally, a set of source documents. It plans the research, gathers cited evidence, reviews its draft, and builds a report bundle.

Choose multiple files or a folder. Up to 20 files, 20 MB each and 50 MB total. Supported: PDF, DOCX, Markdown, TXT, and HTML. Uploaded documents stay on this computer and are indexed separately for this run, alongside ARA's sample corpus.
